The generated outputs show a pattern where the model learns to predict what appears to be an average of all training digits. With an MSE loss, the model learns to predict the point that minimizes the sum of squared distances to all training examples, which results in blurry, averaged representations rather than distinct digits.

After removing the exponential learning rate scheduler and using a constant learning rate of 1e-4, the model maintains similar performance, so I did not need to do anything to compensate. The training remains stable and the generated digits are of comparable quality. This suggests that with proper hyperparameter tuning, a constant learning rate can work just as well for this task.
